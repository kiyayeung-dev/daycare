How to use this app

A free way to find licensed Saskatchewan daycares and keep track of who you’ve called.

1. Set where you live

Type your address in the bar at the top and tap Find — or just tap anywhere on the map to drop your home pin. Then slide Radius to show only daycares within that distance.

2. Find daycares

  • Map tab — tap any dot to open its card.
  • List tab — search by town, daycare name, or address. Misspellings are fine; tap a suggestion.
  • Use the filter chips (Centres, Group home, Infants, etc.) to narrow things down.
  • On a card, tap 📍 Map to jump to the map — that daycare turns into a red dot so you can spot it.

3. Track your calls

Open a daycare and tap Not called / Called / Waitlist / Full. Its dot changes colour so you can see your progress at a glance:

  • Green = not called yet  ·  Yellow = called
  • Blue = on the waitlist  ·  Dark red = full / no spots

Add notes, tap ♥ to save favourites, and check the History and Waitlist tabs to see everything in one place. Tapping Not called again clears that daycare completely.

4. Back up your tracking — important

Your tracking is saved only on this device. There is no account and nothing is sent to us — which keeps it private, but it also means clearing your browser data or deleting the app will erase it. Back it up regularly.
Save a backup file
Tap Download backup below. It saves a small file to your device that contains all your statuses, notes, and favourites.
Email it to yourself
Tap Email my tracking to open your mail app with a readable summary of every daycare you’ve marked. Handy as a record, or to share with a partner.
Restore after clearing your cache
Tap Restore from backup, pick the file you saved, and everything comes back exactly as it was. This also moves your tracking to a new phone.

5. Install it like an app

iPhone: tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.
Android: tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome, then Add to Home screen.

It then opens full-screen with its own icon, and your list works even without signal.

6. Found something wrong?

If a daycare has closed, moved, or its details are wrong — or you’re a provider who’d like your listing removed — please use the feedback form in the About tab. Every message comes straight to us and we’ll fix it.

Always confirm details directly with the daycare. Information here comes from public government listings and can be out of date.

Where the information comes from

Listings are reproduced from a publicly available source — the Government of Saskatchewan's regulated child care registry. For providers with a Parent Central page or their own website, this app links out to those pages rather than reproducing their content. This app makes public information easier to search and organize; it does not create or independently verify it.

Accuracy & official status

In reproducing information from the Government of Saskatchewan's website, this app aims to reproduce it accurately, and it is not — and must not be taken as — an official version of that information. This is an independent tool. If you notice that a daycare listed here has closed, relocated, become temporarily unavailable, or changed in any way, please let us know through the feedback form above so the listing can be corrected.

Your tracking is private to this device

This app has no accounts and no sign-in. Everything you mark — statuses, notes and favourites — is stored only in this browser on this device, and is never sent to us or to anyone else. Because of that, clearing your browser data or deleting the app will erase it. Use Download backup in the ? menu (top right) to save a copy, and Restore from backup to bring it back or move it to a new phone.

Privacy & removal requests

We value your input and respect the privacy of businesses and individuals. If you are a child care provider (or an individual named in a listing) and would like your name or business removed from this app, we will honour your removal request — send it through the feedback form above and it will be actioned promptly.

Please verify before relying on it

Details such as availability, hours, fees, licensing status, and phone numbers change frequently and may be out of date, incomplete, or inaccurate here. Before making any decision, please confirm directly with the child care provider or the official government sources. Distances and travel times shown are estimates only.
